Common questions

Which wearables work with OwlyGo?

Apple Watch, Oura Ring, WHOOP, or any device that saves overnight heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep to Apple Health. You can choose a primary device in Settings. Not every device saves every reading, so your status uses the readings that are there.

Which Apple Watch do I need?

Apple Watch Series 4 or later, or any Apple Watch SE, with watchOS 10 or later. These record the readings your daily status relies on: heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep, plus breathing rate while you sleep. Wrist temperature needs Series 8, Ultra or later; without it, your status uses the other readings. Wear your Watch to bed with sleep tracking turned on.

Today says it's still learning my usual. Why?

OwlyGo compares each night with your own usual, which needs 7 nights of readings. Nights already in Apple Health count. Until then, your weights stay where they are.

My readings are missing.

Check that OwlyGo can read Apple Health: open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then OwlyGo, and turn on the readings. Your Watch or other device also needs to have synced last night's data to your iPhone, which can take a little while after you wake.

How do I change my week or my activities?

Open the Week tab and tap Edit your week. Pick a day, then Strength, Cardio, Flow or Rest. A walk or a run can be outside or on a treadmill.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

OwlyGo's data is included in your iPhone's iCloud Backup unless you turn that off in Settings. You can also use Export my data in Settings, then Import my data on the new iPhone.

How do weights work?

Every weight starts at 0 lb. Type the weight you use on the lift card, and OwlyGo remembers it. A number past the usual most for that equipment, such as 150 lb for dumbbells, is brought down with a note.

Why is there a safety check?

Setup ends with a short list, such as a heart condition or a recent injury. If any applies, OwlyGo plans no workouts and shows only your daily recovery until you tap My doctor says I'm OK in Settings, Health and data, Safety check. Only your answer is kept, never which item applied.

What if I leave a workout open?

After 30 minutes with nothing logged, OwlyGo asks "Still going?". With no answer 15 minutes later, the workout ends at your last activity and is saved. A workout counts once you've logged a set; one ended with nothing logged isn't saved or counted.

Can I see the tour again?

Yes: Settings, Help, Learn and FAQs, Take the tour. It's five short steps, about 30 seconds.

How do I delete my data?

Settings, Health and data, then Delete all data. This removes what OwlyGo keeps on your iPhone, and clears its widget and reminders. Records that you accepted the terms and did the safety check stay, as the privacy policy explains. It doesn't touch Apple Health.
