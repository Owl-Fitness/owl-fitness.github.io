OwlyGo: Privacy policy

Draft, November 17, 2026

OwlyGo is built so your health data stays with you. It has no account, no server and no advertising, and it doesn't use outside analytics or tracking tools. This policy explains what it reads, what it keeps, and when anything leaves your iPhone.

What OwlyGo reads from Apple Health

With your permission, OwlyGo reads sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, breathing rate, wrist temperature, body weight, height, your date of birth and sex, your workouts and the water you drink. These readings can come from your Apple Watch or from another device or app that saves them to Apple Health, such as WHOOP or Oura. OwlyGo notes which device each reading came from, and the primary device you choose in Settings, only on your iPhone. It uses them only to suggest your moves, count your active week and show your insights, on your iPhone, including reading new data in the background as it arrives. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app or in your iPhone's Settings.

What OwlyGo writes to Apple Health

The workouts it records, on your iPhone or Apple Watch, with their heart rate when your Watch measured it, and each cup of water you count on Today, unless you turn that off in Settings. Taking a cup back removes it. Nothing else is written to Apple Health.

What OwlyGo keeps on your iPhone

Your answers from setup and Settings, your plan, the workouts you log, the moves you mark as done, the cups of water you count and your check-ins. These stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch. They aren't sent to the maker of OwlyGo or anyone else, and aren't synced to iCloud. They're included in your iPhone's iCloud Backup if you use it, which Apple encrypts, unless you turn off Include in iCloud Backup in Settings. Export my data in Settings makes a file of them you can keep, and Import my data brings that file back, replacing what's on the iPhone.

The health check

OwlyGo keeps only the health check's outcome, the body areas you chose and the dates, never your answers. A record that the check was done, and your doctor's OK if you gave it, are kept in your iPhone's Keychain, so they remain after you delete your data and go into your iPhone's encrypted iCloud Backup.

When data leaves your iPhone

Only when you choose to share it, such as a card celebrating a workout, a copy of the terms or a file from Export my data, through the iPhone's share sheet. Nothing is sent automatically.

Beta feedback

During the beta, Send feedback in Settings opens an email to the maker with your survey answers, your first name if you leave it in, a random tester ID made on your iPhone, and a summary of how your plan is going: your setup choices, your start date, the date of your first workout, workout counts by week, weight increases, check-in answers, how many workouts had pain reported, and when you accepted the terms. It never includes health readings, your readiness status, your health check answers or outcome, or which body areas hurt. You see all of it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send the email.

Health data is never used for advertising

OwlyGo doesn't show ads, doesn't sell or rent data, and never uses Apple Health data for advertising, marketing or data mining, or shares it with anyone for those purposes.

Subscriptions

Apple handles payment for subscriptions and free trials. OwlyGo learns only whether you have an active subscription or trial, never your payment details.

Information from Apple

If you've agreed to share them with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give the maker of OwlyGo crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. Testers using TestFlight may also send feedback and crash reports to the maker through Apple.

Notifications and spoken cues

OwlyGo's reminders are created on your iPhone, and its spoken cues use voices already on your iPhone. No notification service or server is involved.

The record of accepting the terms

When you accept the terms, your iPhone keeps a record of when, which version, the app version and the type of device. It stays on your iPhone and remains after you delete your data, as evidence that the terms were accepted.

Deleting your data

Delete all data in Settings removes everything OwlyGo keeps, apart from the records kept in the Keychain described above. Deleting the app removes the rest of what it keeps. Data in Apple Health, including workouts OwlyGo saved there, stays under your control in the Health app.

Children

OwlyGo is for people 18 or older and isn't meant for children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears in Settings and on the web page where it's published, with its date.
