Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch to use OwlyGo?

Not necessarily. OwlyGo works with Apple Watch, Oura Ring, WHOOP or any wearable that saves overnight recovery readings (HRV, resting heart rate and sleep) to Apple Health. As long as your wearable syncs to Apple Health, Owly can set your workouts.

Which Apple Watch do I need?

Apple Watch Series 4 or later, or any Apple Watch SE, with watchOS 10 or later. These record the readings your daily status relies on: heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep, plus breathing rate while you sleep. Wrist temperature needs Series 8, Ultra or later; without it, your status uses the other readings. Wear your Watch to bed with sleep tracking turned on.

How does OwlyGo connect to my wearable?

OwlyGo connects to Apple Health on your iPhone with one tap during setup. There's no other setup: Owly reads your synced recovery data every morning.

Can I use OwlyGo if I only work out at home with dumbbells?

Yes. OwlyGo fits your Strength moves to the equipment you have, whether that's a set of home dumbbells, resistance bands or full gym machines.

How do weights work?

You stay in control. Every weight starts at 0 lb: type the weight you use on the lift card, and Owly remembers it. Finish every set and Owly suggests a little more next time. You can change anything, up or down.

Are the workouts based on guidelines?

Yes. OwlyGo's workouts follow the American College of Sports Medicine's guidelines for resistance training: reps, sets and rests suited to your goal and experience. Your suggested week follows the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, with at least 150 active minutes and strength days each week. Training like this may help you get stronger and fitter. OwlyGo is general fitness guidance, not medical advice.

How does OwlyGo help me avoid burnout?

Instead of pushing for maximum effort every day, OwlyGo matches each day's workout to your overnight recovery. On low-recovery days, it shifts Today's Move to a light mobility flow or a rest day.
