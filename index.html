Frequently asked questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Quiet Fitness works with Apple Watch, Oura, WHOOP or any wearable that saves overnight HRV, resting heart rate and sleep to Apple Health. Connect Apple Health once in setup, and Hush reads it every morning.

Which Apple Watch do I need?

Series 4 or later, or any SE, with watchOS 10 or later. Wear it to bed with sleep tracking on. Wrist temperature needs Series 8, Ultra or later, but isn't required.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Quiet Fitness fits each workout to your kit: home dumbbells, bands, kettlebells or a full gym.

How do weights work?

You stay in control. On your first workout, type a weight you can lift for the set's reps with a few to spare, and Hush remembers it. Reach the top of your reps on every set, and Hush offers a little more. You decide.

Are the workouts based on guidelines?

Yes. Quiet Fitness follows the American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM) guidelines for workouts, and your suggested week follows the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans. To check or read more, see ACSM's guidelines for resistance training and the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans. Training like this may help you get stronger and fitter.

How does Quiet Fitness help me avoid burnout?

It matches each day to your overnight recovery. On low-recovery days, Today's move becomes light mobility or rest.

Is Quiet Fitness medical advice?

No. It's general fitness guidance. Talk to your doctor before starting, especially with a health condition, an injury or a pregnancy.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch: no account, no server.
